# User guide
**Run a verification (office):** Verification > New campaign (scope by department/location) > Activate. Track progress on the campaign page; export Excel.
**Verify in the field (Android):** sign in, choose an active campaign, point the camera at the asset QR/barcode, optionally add a photo or comment. Works offline; tap Sync when connected. Unknown tags are recorded as exceptions.
**Desktop:** sign in, pick a campaign, scan or type the tag and press Enter; "Sync now" uploads queued results.
**Labels:** Assets list > QR link opens the asset's QR image.
